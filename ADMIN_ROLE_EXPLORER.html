Defense Defense Rationale: Ceiling vs Nearest-Peso Rounding

Standard commercial rounding (₱499.40 ➔ ₱499.00) would cause an immediate cash deficit during cooperative remittance. BillAlert enforces a mathematical ceiling (ceil): ₱499.01 becomes ₱500.00. The surplus 99 centavos funds the Reserve Fund ("Pondo") and the Meter Reader's ₱500 monthly compensation.

Defense Rationale: Disconnection Window Legal Compliance

Under ERC rules, utilities cannot cut power on weekends or holidays, nor after 3:00 PM (when payment counters close). If notice is served on a Thursday at 2:00 PM, 48 hours lands on Saturday at 2:00 PM. fn_earliest_lawful_disconnection automatically advances the lawful window to Monday at 8:00 AM, protecting the consumer's right to settle.
